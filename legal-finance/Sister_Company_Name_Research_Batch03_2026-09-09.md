# Sister Company Name Research — Batch 03 for "Enable the Disabled" (Not-for-Profit)

**Prepared for:** Enable the Disabled / Shaun Kehoe  
**Date:** September 9, 2026  
**Research Scope:** Canadian federal and provincial corporate registries, trademark database, and active business operations  
**Purpose:** Assess viability of 10 proposed names (Batch 03) for a for-profit sister corporation to the NFP "Enable the Disabled"

---

## Executive Summary

**5 of 10 names** appear clear for Canadian incorporation without conflicts:

| Status | Names |
|--------|-------|
| ✅ **Likely Available** | Adaptive Motion Training, Capability Motion Studio, Access Fitness Training, Ability Athletic Center, Adaptive Athletic Center |
| ⚠️ **Potential Conflicts / Descriptive Risk** | Inclusive Athletic Training (generic/descriptive), Accessible Athletic Center (generic), Adaptive Strength Studio (conflict with Adaptive Strength Association of BC), Accessible Fitness Center (generic/hotel amenity term), Inclusive Fitness Training (generic certification term) |

**Recommendation:** Proceed with **Adaptive Motion Training** or **Adaptive Athletic Center** as primary choices — both have no located Canadian corporate registrations, strong mission fit, and "center" format supports multi-site scaling. **Capability Motion Studio** and **Access Fitness Training** are also clean.
