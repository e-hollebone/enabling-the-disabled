# Sister Company Name Research — Batch 02 for "Enable the Disabled" (Not-for-Profit)

**Prepared for:** Enable the Disabled / Shaun Kehoe  
**Date:** September 9, 2026  
**Research Scope:** Canadian federal and provincial corporate registries, trademark database, and active business operations  
**Purpose:** Assess viability of 10 proposed names (Batch 02) for a for-profit sister corporation to the NFP "Enable the Disabled"

---

## Executive Summary

**4 of 10 names** appear clear for Canadian incorporation without conflicts:

| Status | Names |
|--------|-------|
| ✅ **Likely Available** | Access Athletic Training, Capability Fitness, Accessible Motion Studio, Ability Motion Training, Adaptive Athletic |
| ⚠️ **Potential Conflicts / Descriptive Risk** | Ability Fitness (generic), Inclusive Athletic Studio (descriptive), Inclusive Fitness Training (descriptive/generic), Active Ability Studio (similar to "Active Abilities Canada Inc."), Accessible Strength Training (generic/descriptive) |

**Recommendation:** Proceed with **Access Athletic Training** or **Adaptive Athletic** as primary choices — both have no located Canadian corporate registrations and are distinctive enough for trademark. **Capability Fitness** and **Accessible Motion Studio** are also clean but more descriptive.
