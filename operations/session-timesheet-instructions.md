# Enable the Disabled — Session Time Record: Instructions for Trainers

**Prepared for:** Sean H., Charlotte, and any trainer delivering Enable the Disabled sessions
**Prepared by:** fitness-strategist profile
**Status:** Final — live in Google Drive
**Purpose:** Every session you deliver must be recorded in this timesheet to get paid. If it's not in this sheet, you don't get paid for it.

---

## How This Works (30 seconds)

1. **Fill out one row per session you deliver.** One row = one paid session.
2. **Write the row immediately after the session ends** — same day, while it's fresh.
3. **Shaun checks your row against the calendar** — every entry must match a scheduled appointment. Row matches = you get paid. Row doesn't match = held.
4. **Row 2 is a gray example row** — it shows the format. Start typing on row 3.

---

## What Counts as a Session

A **valid session** is any scheduled personal training or group training appointment that was:

- Booked on the calendar (Shaun assigns via calendar)
- Delivered in person (at gym, in-home, or outdoor)
- At least 50% of the scheduled time completed with the client present

**Late cancellation (under 24h) = still a session.** If a client cancels with less than 24 hours' notice and you're available and prepared, that counts as delivered.

**No-show from you = no pay.**

## Column-by-Column Guide

### Date
When the session happened. Use `YYYY-MM-DD` format, e.g., `2026-09-15`.

### Time
**Start time only.** 24-hour format, e.g., `9:00` or `14:30`. You don't need the end time — just when you started. This is the minimum needed to verify the session happened.

### Client
Who you trained. Must match the name on the calendar booking.

### Type
**Tap the dropdown arrow** and pick one of the five options above. Don't type freehand.

### Results
1–2 sentences on what happened and how the client did. Examples:

- "Upper body strength: 3x10 DB rows @35lb, client hit new rep count"
- "Mobility focus today — client working through shoulder stiffness"
- "Group of 4: completed band circuit, 2 clients set new PRs"

**This can't be blank.** If you just wrote "did workout" or nothing at all, Shaun will hold the row.

---

## Payroll Connection

Every week, Shaun:
1. Filters for the pay period date range
2. Checks each row against the calendar appointment
3. Pays for rows that match

Rows that don't match the calendar or have blank results are held. To avoid delays:
- Fill same day
- Include a real result
- Make sure the client name and date match the calendar

## Mobile Tips

The sheet is designed for phone use. Here's how:

1. Open the **Google Sheets** app on your iPhone or Android
2. Find **"Enable the Disabled - Session Time Record"** (you'll be invited to it)
3. Tap the **+** at the bottom-right of the last data row to add a new row
4. Fill left-to-right:
   - **Date:** tap the cell, type `2026-09-15`
   - **Time:** tap the cell, type `9:00` (24-hour)
   - **Client:** tap and type the name
   - **Type:** tap the cell, then tap the **▼ dropdown arrow** that appears — tap your choice
   - **Results:** tap the cell, tap the **microphone** on your keyboard, speak 1-2 sentences
5. Switch to the **Quick Reference** tab (at the bottom) for a reminder anytime

## Gotchas

1. **No calendar match = no pay.** If the session isn't on the calendar, Shaun can't verify it. Check the calendar before you deliver.

2. **Results can't be blank.** "Did workout" is not a result. Say what you did and how it went.

3. **Same-day entries preferred.** Anything older than 48 hours needs Shaun's OK before payment.

4. **Group sessions** — put "Group: [Client1, Client2]" in the Client column.

5. **Substituting for another trainer?** Note it in Results: "Covering for Shaun — John P. session."
