# Enabling the Disabled

Business documentation for a personal training + gym delivery fitness business serving disabled and handicapped clients in Ottawa, Ontario.

## What We Know

- **Owner:** Shaun Kehoe, 15+ years experience, Autism Exercise Specialist Certified
- **Mission:** Show people with disabilities they can do more than the world told them
- **Revenue:** ~$50K/year, bootstrapped, sole proprietorship → moving to corporation + nonprofit
- **Team:** 1.5 FTE (Shaun + 2 part-time contractors: Sean, Charlotte)
- **Clients:** All ages, all abilities. ~70% disabled clients, ~30% high school athletes
- **Facilities:** Point One Fitness (rented, $1,500/mo), home visits, home gym setup
- **Geography:** Ottawa region, ~1 hour travel radius

## What We Discovered

- **Strengths:** Passionate delivery, strong community ties, authentic social proof, active Instagram
- **Weaknesses:** Broken website contact forms (revenue leak), no pricing online, no booking system, zero operational documentation, ad hoc sales
- **Opportunities:** Government funding channel, untapped referral network (physiotherapists, occupational therapist), radio station celebrity connection
- **Biggest gap:** Shaun is trapped in tactical work — needs operational structure to free him up to sell
