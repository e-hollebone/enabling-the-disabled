# Current State Assessment

**Status:** Not started — waits for business context collection  
**Owner:** TBD  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-27

## Purpose

Once we have the business context collected, this document becomes the formal assessment of where the business actually stands. It's the bridge between "what we know" and "what we produce next."

## Structure (to be filled after collection)

### 1. What exists today

A clear, honest picture of the business as it stands — clients, offerings, assets, presence, anything already in motion.

### 2. What's missing

The gaps between where the business is and where it needs to be to operate cleanly and grow.

### 3. What's working

Strengths and assets to build on — don't start from zero when something already works.

### 4. Priorities

A ranked set of next steps, with the reasoning behind the ranking. This is what the documents we produce should serve.

### 5. Risks / constraints

Anything that limits what we can do or how fast — legal, financial, operational, temporal.

## How this is used

The assessment feeds every document that follows. If a document proposes something that contradicts the assessment, the assessment wins until it's updated.
