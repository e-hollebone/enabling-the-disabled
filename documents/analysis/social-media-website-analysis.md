## Website — shaunkehoe.com

### Strengths

| Area | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| **Clear positioning** | "Adaptive Personal Training & Disability Fitness Programs in Ottawa" — immediately clear what it is and who it's for |
| **Strong mission statement** | "I am on a life mission to show people with disabilities that they can do so much more than what the world tried to tell them." — emotional, authentic, differentiating |
| **Social proof** | 6 testimonials on the site, all specific and emotional (mentions of cerebral palsy, autism, non-verbal clients) |
| **Community partners** | 5 partner logos displayed (Epilepsy Ottawa, Capital City Condors, BIAOV, Fostering Forever Friendships, Tamir Foundation) — builds credibility |
| **Service clarity** | 4 distinct services listed (Personal, Group, Adaptive, Athletic) with "Learn More" links |
| **Call to action** | "START YOUR JOURNEY" button prominently placed |
| **Experience signal** | "15+ years experience" stated upfront |
| **Visual storytelling** | "STYLE GALLERY" section with community photos — "So much more than fitness. This is a COMMUNITY." |

### Weaknesses

| Area | Observation | Severity |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Broken contact form** | "START YOUR JOURNEY" links to contact page, but form shows "Please select a valid form" — this is a revenue leak. Potential clients can't reach Shaun. | 🔴 Critical |
| **Broken "FREE HOME EVALUATION"** | Same issue — form doesn't work. This is a key conversion point for in-home clients. | 🔴 Critical |
| **No pricing** | No mention of session rates, packages, or even a price range. Visitors must contact to find out — friction that loses leads. | 🟡 Significant |
| **No booking system** | No online scheduling. All booking requires manual contact. | 🟡 Significant |
| **Testimonials duplicated** | Same 3 testimonials appear to be repeated — reduces credibility. | 🟢 Moderate |
| **No blog / content hub** | No articles, resources, or educational content. Missed SEO opportunity and thought leadership platform. | 🟢 Moderate |
| **No clear next step** | "LET'S CHAT" and "SEE MORE SUCCESS STORIES" — but no structured onboarding flow. | 🟢 Moderate |
| **SEO basics missing** | No visible meta description, no blog for organic traffic, no local SEO optimization (Google Business Profile not mentioned). | 🟢 Moderate |
| **No accessibility features** | For a business serving disabled clients, no visible accessibility tools (screen reader optimization, alt text on images, etc.). | 🟡 Significant |
| **No team page** | Shaun is the only person mentioned. No page for the other Sean or Charlotte — missed trust-building opportunity. | 🟢 Moderate |
| **No FAQ** | Common questions (what to expect, what to bring, cancellation policy, etc.) not answered on the site. | 🟢 Moderate |
